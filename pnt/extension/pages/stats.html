Postflop columns follow the street you pick. Hover a column header or open the key below the table for what each one counts.

  • C-Bet, Fold, Raise and Lead are measured against the previous street's aggressor. Agg % is not: it counts every action on the street.
  • Fold vs C-Bet + Raise C-Bet + calls = 100% of the c-bets faced.
  • A lead takes the street out of the c-bet columns: no c-bet chance for the aggressor, and no fold or raise for anyone else.
  • A blank rate means they never reached the spot. Hover a rate for its sample size.
