All-in EV what the jams were worth, against what they took

stats › range chart › players ›

Every all-in showdown where every live hand was shown. Adjusted is what the player's equity was worth when the betting stopped; Actual is what they took; the Diff is the deck's contribution.

  • Positive diff ran above expectation, negative below. Within one hand the diffs cancel: nobody's luck comes from nowhere.
  • Flop, turn and river all-ins are enumerated exactly. Preflop is sampled (50,000 deals, marked ~ in the hand list). A river all-in has cards to come, so its diff is 0.
  • Hands where a live player mucked are counted as skipped, not guessed at. All-ins nobody called are not showdowns and are not here.
  • A dozen jams say almost nothing; the diff swings by a whole stack per hand.
  • Click a player for their cumulative actual and adjusted lines and the hands behind them.

What the columns mean
Hands
All-in showdowns they were part of and still in at the end, with every live hand known.
Eq%
Their average share of the pot at the moment the betting stopped.
Actual
Net chips over those hands, the same figure every other page prints.
Adjusted
Net chips had every pot been split by equity instead of dealt out.
Diff
Actual minus adjusted. Above zero ran good, below ran bad.
Preflop … River
The diff split by the street the betting stopped on. Hover for that street's hands, actual and adjusted.
~
In the hand list: a preflop equity, sampled rather than enumerated.
